Good to know

Keep it simple.

A good tradition should be easy to join. Start wherever you are.

Do I need a garden?

No. A pot or window box can be a place to begin. Choose a plant that suits your light and growing conditions.

Which flower should I choose?

Look for a flower that grows well where you live and is useful to local pollinators. A local nursery or native-plant guide can help you choose.

Can my school or community join?

Absolutely. Choose a shared spot together, ask the people responsible for it, and make planting day a small community celebration.

Does my plan get posted publicly?

No. Your plan is saved in your browser on your device. It isn’t sent to a server or shared unless you choose to share it.

HERE WHEN YOU NEED US

Help & garden tips.

A few answers to keep your next step simple.

What is Bloom for Bees Day?

A yearly invitation to add one pollinator-friendly flower to a place you can care for, and invite someone to grow with you. It’s celebrated on June 7.

How do I choose a flower?

Look for plants that suit your local climate, sunlight, and growing space. A local native-plant guide or nursery can help identify flowers that support pollinators near you.

Are my garden plans public?

No. Your account and garden plans are private. Your garden data is stored on this device’s local server and is not published publicly.

Can I use this without a garden?

Yes. A pot, window box, school space, or community garden can all be a starting place. Ask before planting in a shared space.

What happens to my garden when I sign out?

Your garden stays saved with your account. Sign back in on this same local app to continue.
